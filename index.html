RotMG Progression Guide →

Plan your gear in Realm of the Mad God, class by class. It suggests the next weapon, ability, armor and ring to aim for, balancing damage against how hard each item is to get.

  • Weapon DPS from each class's max stats, including armor piercing, multi-shot and burst weapons
  • Difficulty from the dungeon each item drops in, with extra weight for rare biome and event whites
  • A checklist that saves the items you've got and hides what you've outgrown
